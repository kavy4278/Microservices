# Crumbly — Stakeholder Financial Benefits & Value Proposition

**Product:** Crumbly — Surplus Food Rescue Network

**Purpose:** Connect donors, delivery riders, hotels, cloud kitchens, food shops, Gruh Udyog businesses, PGs, hostels, and NGOs to reduce food waste, create earning opportunities, and provide quality food to communities in need.

---

## Platform Policy & Implementation Considerations

- **Transparent Rewards:** Define star-earning rules, donation streaks, redemption rates, and eligible reward items.
- **Fair Rider Payments:** Establish clear delivery rates, payment schedules, and eligibility for performance incentives.
- **Flexible Business Participation:** Allow food businesses to choose between paid orders and voluntary contributions.
- **Food Safety:** Ensure all donated food meets applicable food-safety requirements and is suitable for consumption.
- **City-Wide Coverage:** Enable PGs, hostels, NGOs, food providers, and riders to connect within their service areas as the network expands.
- **Accountability:** Maintain appropriate records of food listings, collections, deliveries, rewards, and payments.

**Core Mission:** Transform surplus food into a shared economic and social opportunity by rewarding donors, creating employment, supporting local businesses, reducing food waste, and helping NGOs deliver quality meals to people who need them.
